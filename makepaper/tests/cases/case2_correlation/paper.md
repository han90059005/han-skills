# 退火处理对 PVA 薄膜拉伸强度的影响

**作者**：Test C
**年份**：2024
**期刊**：Journal of Hypothetical Polymer Science
**来源类型**：期刊论文
**证据级别**：摘要级（仅摘要，未获取全文）

## 摘要

Annealing at 120 °C for 2 h increased the tensile strength of PVA films by 40% (from 25 to 35 MPa). The improvement is attributed to the reorganization of hydrogen-bonding networks, which is proposed to facilitate stress transfer between chains.
