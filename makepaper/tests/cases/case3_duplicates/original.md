# Flexible Ag2Se thermoelectric films with high power factor

**作者**：Li X, Zhang Y
**年份**：2023
**期刊**：Journal of Hypothetical Energy Materials
**来源类型**：期刊论文
**证据级别**：摘要级

## 摘要

We report flexible Ag2Se films grown on nylon substrates. The films exhibit a power factor of 1200 μW m−1 K−2 and ZT of 0.6 at 300 K. The thermoelectric performance is attributed to the high carrier mobility of the Ag2Se phase.
