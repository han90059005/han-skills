# Recent advances in flexible thermoelectric materials

**作者**：Wang Q
**年份**：2024
**期刊**：Hypothetical Reviews in Materials
**来源类型**：综述
**证据级别**：摘要级

## 摘要

This review summarizes flexible thermoelectric materials. Li et al. reported Ag2Se films with a power factor of 1200 μW m−1 K−2 and ZT of 0.6 at 300 K. The review discusses strategies for improving performance.
