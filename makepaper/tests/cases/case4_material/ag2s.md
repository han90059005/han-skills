# Ag2S film thermoelectric performance under sulfur atmosphere

**作者**：Test E
**年份**：2022
**来源类型**：期刊论文
**证据级别**：摘要级

## 摘要

Ag2S films deposited under a sulfur-rich atmosphere show an electrical conductivity of 200 S cm−1 and a Seebeck coefficient of −300 μV K−1 at 300 K, giving a power factor of 18 μW m−1 K−2. The measurement was performed after 1 h of sulfurization at 150 °C.
