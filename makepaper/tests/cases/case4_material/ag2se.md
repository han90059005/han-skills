# Ag2Se flexible film thermoelectric performance

**作者**：Test D
**年份**：2023
**来源类型**：期刊论文
**证据级别**：摘要级

## 摘要

Ag2Se films on nylon show an electrical conductivity of 800 S cm−1 and a Seebeck coefficient of −150 μV K−1 at 300 K, giving a power factor of 18 μW m−1 K−2. The film was annealed at 200 °C for 30 min.
