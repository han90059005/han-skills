# Annealing-induced strength improvement in composite films

**作者**：Test F, Test G
**年份**：2024
**来源类型**：期刊论文
**证据级别**：摘要级

## 摘要

Annealing at 120 °C for 2 h increased the tensile strength of composite films from 25 to 35 MPa (40% improvement). We attribute the improvement to annealing-induced chain alignment.
