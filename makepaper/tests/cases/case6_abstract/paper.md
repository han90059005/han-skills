# 包覆复合膜的循环稳定性

**作者**：Test I
**年份**：2025
**来源类型**：期刊论文
**证据级别**：摘要级（仅摘要）

## 摘要

A coated composite film showed improved cycling stability compared with the uncoated baseline. The capacity retention after 200 cycles was higher than that of the baseline. The authors propose that the coating suppresses side reactions at the electrode–electrolyte interface.
